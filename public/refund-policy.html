2. Refund Eligibility

Refund requests may be considered only if all applicable eligibility criteria are met.

A. Training Programs & Certification Courses

Participants may request a refund if:

The refund request is submitted within 7 calendar days of purchase; and

The participant has not completed more than 20% of the course content; and

No certification has been issued; and

No course completion certificate has been generated.

Once a participant has completed substantial portions of the course, attended live sessions, downloaded learning materials, or received certification, the program becomes non-refundable.

 

B. Workshops, Seminars & Events

Refund requests for workshops, webinars, bootcamps, conferences, and events may be eligible only if:

The cancellation request is submitted at least 7 days before the scheduled event date.

Approved refunds may be subject to administrative processing charges.

No refunds will be provided for:

No-shows

Missed sessions

Partial attendance

Event recordings already accessed

 

C. Software Products & SaaS Subscriptions

Refunds for software subscriptions may be considered if:

The request is made within 14 days of the initial subscription purchase; and

The service has not been substantially utilized; and

No violation of platform policies has occurred.

Subscription fees already consumed during the service period are non-refundable.

Monthly, quarterly, and annual subscription plans automatically renew unless canceled before the next billing cycle.

 

D. Consulting & Professional Services

Consulting, implementation, development, digital marketing, AI automation, website development, and professional services are generally non-refundable once work has commenced.

Refund requests may only be considered where:

NXDigita Technologies is unable to commence the agreed project; or

Services cannot be delivered due to circumstances solely attributable to NXDigita Technologies.

Any completed milestones, deliverables, or project phases remain chargeable.

 

E. Internship, Recruitment & Talent Verification Programs

Fees paid for:

Assessments

Candidate verification

Talent evaluation

Internship programs

Performance verification services

Recruitment services

Talenttopper platform participation

are generally non-refundable once the candidate assessment, onboarding process, verification process, evaluation process, or program participation has commenced.

4. Cancellation Policy

A. Subscription Cancellation

Users may cancel recurring subscriptions at any time through their account settings or by contacting our support team.

Cancellation will:

Prevent future billing cycles; and

Allow continued access until the current subscription period expires.

No partial refunds will be provided for unused subscription periods.

 

B. Program Cancellation by Participant

Participants who cancel their enrollment before the program commencement date may be eligible for a refund subject to applicable processing fees and eligibility conditions.

 

C. Program Cancellation by NXDigita Technologies

NXDigita Technologies reserves the right to:

Reschedule programs

Modify program delivery formats

Cancel events due to operational, technical, safety, or business reasons

Where a program is canceled by NXDigita Technologies and no alternative arrangement is provided, eligible participants may receive either:

A full refund; or

Credit toward a future program

at NXDigita Technologies' discretion.

 

5. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please follow the steps below:

Step 1: Contact Support

Submit your refund request by email with:

Full Name

Registered Email Address

Transaction ID

Invoice Number (if applicable)

Program or Service Name

Detailed reason for refund request

Step 2: Review Process

Our support team will review the request and may request additional documentation where necessary.

Review timelines may take up to 10 business days.

Step 3: Approval & Processing

If approved, refunds will be processed within 10–15 business days using the original payment method wherever possible.

Processing times may vary depending on banks, payment gateways, and financial institutions.
